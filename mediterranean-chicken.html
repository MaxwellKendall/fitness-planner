Mediterranean Chicken

Week of May 18 · batch cook · 5 servings
Nutrition — per serving
480 kcal
52g Protein
22g Carbs

5 Servings

52g protein per serving — pair with rice or potatoes to hit your 2,600 kcal training day target. Good for lunch or dinner Mon–Fri.

Ingredients

Chicken

Vegetables (roast alongside)

Instructions — Saturday batch cook (~45 min active)
1

Marinate — Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, oregano, paprika, cumin, cinnamon, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Add chicken and coat well. Marinate at least 30 min (overnight in the fridge is better — prep Friday night).

2

Preheat oven to 425°F. Line two large sheet pans with foil.

3

Arrange — Place chicken on one pan. Toss vegetables with a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper and spread on the second pan.

4

Roast — 25–30 min until chicken is cooked through (internal temp 165°F) and vegetables are caramelized. Rotate pans halfway through.

5

Finish — Let chicken rest 5 min, then slice or shred. Combine with roasted vegetables, scatter feta over the top. Store in 5 containers.
