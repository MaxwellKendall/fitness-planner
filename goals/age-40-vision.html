How Today's Training Ladders In
  • Recomp first. A lean 180 lb makes every relative-strength and cardio target easier for the rest of the journey — it's the highest-leverage move right now.
  • Add the aerobic base now. Your plan has cardio as "optional" — for longevity it becomes a co-equal pillar. Start with 2× Zone 2 sessions/week around the lifting.
  • Your current lifts are already near the age-40 strength targets — so from here, strength is about consistency and joint health, not chasing bigger maxes. The growth areas are cardio, grip, balance, and leanness.
  • Consistency > intensity. Four years of logged, sustainable RPE-7 work is exactly the longevity formula.
Longevity Guardrails (build these habits now)
  • Never true-max. Test strength with rep-work at RPE 7, not 1RM attempts. This is the core longevity trade-off.
  • Deload discipline. Every 6 weeks now → every 5 at 40. It's what lets you train for decades without breaking down.
  • Daily mobility + weekly balance. Being pain-free at 38 is the best predictor of still lifting at 48.
  • Protein & sleep. 160 g protein and 7+ hrs — non-negotiable for muscle retention and recovery.
  • Measure it. Run /log after every session; re-test the six pillars each quarter.
