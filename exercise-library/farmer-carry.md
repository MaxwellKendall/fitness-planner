## Cues
- Pick up with a deadlift pattern — hinge, grip, brace, stand. Do not round to grab the floor.
- Stand tall: shoulders packed down and back, chin level. Do not lean forward or tilt to either side.
- Short, controlled steps — heel strike → midfoot → toe-off. Keep the hips level; do not waddle.
- Breathing: small sips of air while braced. Do not fully relax the core mid-carry.
- Set down with control using the same hinge pattern. Do not drop unless using bumper plates.

## Programming Notes
Carry pattern was absent from training prior to week 1 (trend: undertrained). Introduces without
spike in session fatigue — low technique demand, easy to add as a finisher. Start at 22 kg/hand
(~49 lb) for 30 m × 3 sets and progress load or distance weekly. Suitcase carry (one arm only)
can substitute to add anti-lateral-flexion demand for core development. Do not superset with
other grip-intensive exercises (pull-ups, rows) — let grip recover between.

## Warm-up Protocol
No warm-up set needed. Grip the handles, brace, and go. Lighter first set (75% load) if needed.
