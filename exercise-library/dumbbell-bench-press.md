## Cues
- Lie on a bench or floor. DBs at chest level, elbows at ~45 degrees from torso (not flared 90).
- Brace your core and press your feet into the floor.
- Press the DBs up and slightly together — not straight up — to maximize pec engagement.
- Control the descent for 2 seconds; do not bounce off the chest.
- Floor press variation: forearms hit the floor at the bottom — shorter ROM but safer for
  shoulders when no bench is available.

## Programming Notes
The DB press is the primary horizontal push for home gym setups. The Tier 30–39 target is
0.3× BW per hand × 8 reps (e.g., 24kg per hand for an 80kg athlete). Adjust targets to
available dumbbell increments. Sets of 6–10 are effective across strength and hypertrophy
goals. If training on the floor (no bench), use dumbbell-floor-press as the substitute — it
reduces shoulder stress and is equally effective for the push pattern.

## Warm-up Protocol
Light DBs × 10 reps (focus on shoulder retraction), then moderate DBs × 5, then working weight.
