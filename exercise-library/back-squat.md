## Cues
- Bar position: high bar rests on upper traps; low bar sits across rear delts. Use high bar as default.
- Unrack with a full breath into the belly. Brace before you step back, not after.
- Feet shoulder-width or slightly wider; toes out 20–30 degrees. Stance is individual — find yours.
- Descend by sitting between the knees, not by pushing the butt back first. Elbows stay under the bar.
- Depth: crease of the hip at or below the top of the knee. No partial reps.
- Drive out of the hole through the whole foot. Think "spread the floor" not just "push up."
- On the way up, hips and shoulders rise at the same rate — don't let the hips shoot up first.

## Programming Notes
Tier 30–39 barbell target: 1.25× BW (250 lb at 200 lb bodyweight). Re-establish current capacity
in weeks 1–3 using conservative loads (RPE 6–7, 3–5 sets of 5 reps). Run working sets through
Brzycki to estimate 1RM once form is solid. Powerbuilding split: squat on Session A days (low-rep
compound), goblet squat can substitute for warm-up or accessory work. Do not squat and deadlift
heavy in the same session — separate by at least one day.

## Warm-up Protocol
Hip circles × 10, bodyweight squat × 10 (pause 2 sec at bottom), then barbell:
1 × 10 @ empty bar, 1 × 5 @ 50% working weight, 1 × 5 @ 75% working weight, then top sets.
