## Cues
- Brace one hand and one knee on a bench or use a staggered stance.
- DB hangs straight down from the shoulder, arm fully extended.
- Row elbow past the hip — not out to the side. Think "put your elbow in your back pocket."
- Pause briefly at the top; don't shrug the shoulder or rotate excessively.
- Lower with control. Keep the core braced throughout — no twisting for momentum.

## Programming Notes
Pairs with dumbbell bench press to balance horizontal push and pull on the same session.
Target 1:1 push:pull set ratio (or slightly more pull) to protect shoulder health over time.
Sets of 8–12 work well. Heavier loads (5–8 reps) develop mid-back thickness. Because it's
unilateral, it also trains anti-rotation core stability. Counts as a pull-pattern set in
session logs (each arm = 1 set).

## Warm-up Protocol
Scapular retractions (no weight) × 10, then light DB × 8, then working weight.
