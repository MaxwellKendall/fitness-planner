## Cues
- Hold DBs in front of thighs, overhand grip. Stand tall — shoulders back, chest up.
- Push hips back first, not down. The movement is a hinge, not a squat.
- Maintain a slight knee bend throughout — do not let knees travel forward.
- Lower until you feel a strong hamstring stretch (typically mid-shin). Back stays neutral.
- Drive hips forward to stand — squeeze glutes at the top, don't hyperextend.

## Programming Notes
Primary hinge pattern for dumbbell setups. The 0.4× BW per hand target (Tier 30–39) means
roughly 32kg per hand for an 80kg athlete — calibrate to what's available. Sets of 8–10 are
the sweet spot for hamstring development; sets of 4–6 heavier build posterior chain strength.
Easier on the lower back than conventional deadlift from the floor — appropriate for most
injury profiles. Pair with goblet squat in the same session for a complete lower day.

## Warm-up Protocol
Hip hinge pattern with dowel or PVC × 10, then light DB RDL × 8, then working weight.
