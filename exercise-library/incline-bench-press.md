## Cues
- Bench set to 30–45° incline — steeper shifts more load to the front delts, flatter keeps more on the chest.
- Same 5-point setup as flat bench: feet flat, glutes and upper back on the bench, scapula retracted and depressed.
- Grip slightly wider than shoulder-width. Bar path touches higher on the chest (upper chest/clavicle line) than flat bench.
- Elbows tucked 45–75°, same as flat bench — don't flare to compensate for the angle.
- Expect to move meaningfully less weight than flat bench at the same RPE — this is a different lift, not a percentage of flat bench.
