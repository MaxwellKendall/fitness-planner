## Cues
- Bar over mid-foot (1 inch from shin). Hips hinge back to grip — do not squat down to the bar.
- Grip just outside the legs. Double overhand until grip is the limiting factor, then use mixed or hook.
- Before pulling: chest up ("proud chest"), lats engaged ("protect your armpits"), breath and brace.
- Initiate by pushing the floor away — think leg press first, then hinge. Do not yank.
- Bar stays in contact with the legs the entire pull. It should scrape the shin and drag up the thigh.
- Lock out by squeezing glutes and standing tall — do not hyperextend the lumbar at the top.
- Lower under control (hinge back first, then bend knees) or do a controlled drop from lockout.

## Programming Notes
Tier 30–39 target: 1.75× BW deadlift (350 lb at 200 lb bodyweight). Historical PR 255 lb is the
biggest long-term strength gap. Re-establish starting at RPE 6 with conservative loads (weeks 1–3).
Deadlift on Session B days only — never pair heavy squat + heavy deadlift in the same session.
Fatigue cost is high (5/5): limit to 3–5 working sets, do not add excessive volume early in a block.
Warm-up sets count toward total session fatigue; include them in weekly set tracking.

## Warm-up Protocol
Hip hinge drill with dowel rod or broomstick × 10, then barbell:
1 × 5 @ 50% working weight, 1 × 3 @ 75% working weight, then top sets.
