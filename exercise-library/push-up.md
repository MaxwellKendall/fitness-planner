## Cues
- Hands slightly wider than shoulder-width. Elbows track at ~45 degrees — not flared out.
- Body forms a rigid plank from head to heels — squeeze glutes and abs before descending.
- Chest touches or nearly touches the floor at the bottom. Do not short-rep.
- Press up explosively; control the descent for 2 seconds.
- For easier variation: hands elevated on a surface reduces load. For harder: feet elevated.

## Programming Notes
The Tier 30–39 push goal is 15 unbroken full push-ups (chest to floor, full extension).
Progress by tracking max unbroken reps each week and aiming to add 1–2 reps per week.
When current max is ≥ 12, begin adding resistance (weight vest, plate on back) to continue
progression. Counts as a push-pattern set. Push-ups and dumbbell bench press target the same
pattern — use them interchangeably or combine in the same session without doubling the push
volume beyond the weekly target.

## Warm-up Protocol
5 slow-tempo push-ups (3 sec down, 1 sec pause) before working sets.
