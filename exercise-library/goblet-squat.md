## Cues
- Hold the dumbbell vertically at chest height, both hands cupping the top end.
- Feet shoulder-width or slightly wider; toes turned out 15–30 degrees.
- Brace hard before descending — ribs down, belt tight.
- Sit between your knees, not behind them. Elbows track inside the knees at the bottom.
- Drive through the whole foot — not just the heel — on the way up.

## Programming Notes
Primary squat-pattern movement for home gym and dumbbell-only setups. Works at any rep range:
sets of 5–6 at a heavy DB for strength development; sets of 10–15 at moderate load for
hypertrophy or metabolic work. A reliable 1.25× BW squat proxy when barbell is unavailable —
test with the heaviest available dumbbell for 8 clean reps. Pairs well with Romanian deadlift
in the same session; keep total compound sets ≤ 8 if pairing.

## Warm-up Protocol
Bodyweight squat × 10 (pause 2 sec at bottom), then light DB goblet squat × 8, then working weight.
