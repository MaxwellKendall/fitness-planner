## Cues
- Dead hang start — shoulders fully elevated, arms straight. Do not kip or swing.
- Initiate by depressing the scapula (shoulders down and back) before pulling.
- Pull elbows toward your back pockets, not just down. Drive the chest toward the bar.
- Chin clears the bar at the top. Control the descent — 2–3 seconds down.
- Full extension at the bottom before the next rep. No half-rep bunny hops.

## Programming Notes
The pull goal for Tier 30–39 is 10 unbroken pull-ups (from dead hang). Progress by adding one
rep every 1–2 weeks, or by adding load (weight vest or dumbbell between feet) once 8+ clean
reps are consistent. Work sets of 3–5 reps (2 reps short of failure) outperform grinding sets
to failure for long-term development. If current max is < 5, use band-assisted-pull-up as the
primary movement and accumulate volume there first. Never count partial reps toward the goal.

## Warm-up Protocol
Dead hang × 20 sec, scapular pulls × 5 (no elbow bend), then 2–3 easy warm-up reps.
