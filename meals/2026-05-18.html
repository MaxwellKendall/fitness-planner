Saturday May 16 — Bulk Cook (today, ~2 hours)

Greek Chicken Tzatziki Bowls

6 servings protein 4/5 nutrition 4/5 effort 3/5

Covers Mon–Fri lunches. Marinate 15 min, bake 25 min. Assemble bowls with feta, tzatziki, cucumber, avocado, dill.

Cuban Chicken & Black Bean Quinoa Bowls

4 servings protein 4/5 nutrition 4/5 effort 2/5

Covers Mon–Wed dinners + 1 leftover. Chicken marinates 20 min while quinoa cooks. Fried bananas and mango salsa on the side.

Prep order

  1. Marinate tzatziki chicken (yogurt + spices) → refrigerate. Marinate Cuban chicken (citrus + cumin) → refrigerate.
  2. Start quinoa on stovetop (15 min hands-off). Cook black beans.
  3. While quinoa cooks: fry bananas and make mango salsa for Cuban bowls.
  4. Bake tzatziki chicken at 425°F (15 min, toss, 5–10 min more, 1–2 min broil).
  5. Sear Cuban chicken in skillet. Assemble Cuban bowls → portion into 4 containers.
  6. Assemble tzatziki bowls (chicken + cucumber + avocado + feta + dill) → portion into 6 containers.
Wednesday May 20 — Midweek Cook (~1 hour)

Easy Baked Chicken & Zucchini

4 servings protein 4/5 nutrition 4/5 effort 3/5

Covers Thu–Fri dinners. Toss in spiced marinade, bake at 425°F for 30 min. Prep takes 5 min. Fits in 1-hr window with room to spare.

Prep steps

  1. Cut chicken breast into bite-size pieces. Slice zucchini. Chop onion.
  2. Mix spices with olive oil. Toss all ingredients in marinade.
  3. Spread on baking dish. Bake at 425°F for 30 min uncovered.
  4. Cool and portion into 4 containers (2 dinner, 2 extra).
