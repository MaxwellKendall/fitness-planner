Ordering Strategy
  1. Anchor every meal on a named protein portion. Look for a stated protein (chicken, fish, steak, eggs, tofu, Greek yogurt) rather than a dish name — "grilled" or "roasted" beats "fried" or "breaded" for hitting the target without extra calories.
  2. Order protein first, sides second. Pick the protein, then choose one starch/veg side rather than a dish that's mostly sauce and carbs.
  3. Breakfast is the easiest win. Eggs (any style) or a Greek yogurt bowl gets 25–35g of protein before the day gets unpredictable.
  4. Airbnb has a fridge even without a kitchen. Stock it once: Greek yogurt, hard-boiled eggs, string cheese, deli turkey, protein bars, rotisserie chicken if a grocery store is nearby. No cooking required — this covers the gap between restaurant meals.
  5. Alcohol and dessert — moderate, don't eliminate. It's vacation. Pick one or the other on a given day rather than both, and treat both as separate from the protein target, not a substitute for it.
  6. Hydrate deliberately. Travel days and heat (tennis) both increase fluid needs faster than thirst signals it — water with every meal, more on tennis days.
