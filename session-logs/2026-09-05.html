First fully clean loaded squat session in five weeks. Back squat 155 lb × 5 × 4 with zero adductor/groin sensation during or after. That's the outcome the whole gated ladder was built to find, and it arrived in one step instead of three. The right adductor has been the limiting constraint on this program since 2026-07-14 — this is the first real evidence it may be behind you.
Ran well above the plan, with the gates skipped. Programmed: an unloaded movement screen, then a goblet ramp, then 2 sets of 5 at 95 lb only if both were clean. It came back completely clean, which is genuinely good news and real information. Worth being precise about what it does and doesn't tell you, though: the plan's stop-rules were never about the sets hurting during the session — they were about the 24–48 hour delayed response, which is the signal that's been missed on every previous flare of this injury. That reading is still pending. Check the adductor Sunday and Monday morning and log it.
